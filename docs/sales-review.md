## Customer Review｜客户经营复盘

**复盘对象 / Review Scope**<br>已成交客户 Existing customers

<br>** 复盘重点 / Review Focus**

- **Sell-in：H1出货表现**<br> H1 shipment performance 
- **Sell-out：H1激活与终端消化情况**<br> H1 activation and sell-out performance 
- **客户状态与主要问题**<br> Customer status and key issues 
- **Q3 / H2预计出货与关键动作**<br> Q3 / H2 expected shipment and key actions 

**复盘目的 / Review Purpose**<br> 判断客户上半年经营是否健康，以及下半年是否还能继续贡献销售。<br> To evaluate whether the customer performed healthily in H1 and whether they can continue contributing shipments in H2.

> 原始业务表已替换为文末的空白模板，未导出客户行数据。

## BD Review｜招商复盘

**复盘对象 / Review Scope**<br> 新客户 /  新渠道机会<br> New customers / new channel opportunities

** 复盘重点 / Review Focus**

- **招商阶段**<br> BD stage 
- **H1实际出货情况**<br> H1 actual shipment 
- **H2预计出货机会**<br> H2 expected shipment opportunity 
- **当前卡点与下一步动作**<br> Key blockers and next actions 

**复盘目的 / Review Purpose**<br> 判断新客户推进到哪一步，是否已转化，以及下半年能否形成实际出货。<br> To evaluate the current BD progress, whether the lead has been converted, and whether it can generate actual shipment in H2.

> 原始业务表已替换为文末的空白模板，未导出客户行数据。
